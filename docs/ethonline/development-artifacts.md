# Development specifications, prompts and plans

## Provenance and coverage

The Task 36 and Task 40 documents below were published during development. They retain their historical contents and are now explicitly labeled as historical.

The additional component specifications were prepared on 12 September during finalization. They are retrospective public reconstructions from reviewed requirements and the shipped implementation, not verbatim original prompts or contemporaneous execution logs. They preserve the public technical contracts and identify important changes to earlier assumptions. Private operations and unrelated trading work are excluded.

This distinction matters: the collection does not claim to be a complete export of all original specifications, prompts, plans or conversations. An independent privacy review read all 31 sanitized internal source drafts. Direct publication was not cleared: removing secret values and deployment paths alone does not remove private operations, unrelated trading context or misleading historical instructions. Public historical editions would require document-specific editing and review. Complete original-artifact coverage remains a submission-review item; these reconstructions do not silently satisfy that requirement by themselves.
